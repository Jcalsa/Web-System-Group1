About Me

Hello! I am John Michael Calsa, but you can call me JM, and I like to sit, relax and smell the flowers as I watch the world move.

I love relaxing beside the flowers because it helps me calm down as the world changes around me.

My Floral Favorites

Forget-me-not

Forget-me-nots symbolizes remembrance, and as someone who likes to watch stuff, I can understand the feeling of wanting to remember the things I hold dear.

Candytuft

Candytufts symbolizes indifference, and I pick this as one of faves is because, as I watch, I feel indifferent because I accept that things changes whether we like it or not.
