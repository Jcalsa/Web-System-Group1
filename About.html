About Us

Welcome to Petal & Bloom. We are a neighborhood flower studio creating hand-tied bouquets and thoughtful arrangements for birthdays, anniversaries, graduations, sympathy flowers, and everyday surprises.

Each order is prepared with fresh seasonal blooms, careful wrapping, and a personalized message card so every bouquet feels meaningful from the moment it arrives.

We focus on reliable support, easy ordering, and on-time local delivery to help you celebrate important moments with confidence.
